# Stage 4: Levels

## Goal
Add more places to explore, with challenge that builds gently: each level adds one new idea, and stars unlock levels.

## Player experience
- The title screen's **Play** opens a **level select**: cards for each level with its name, a small picture, the friend target, best stars and time, and a padlock with the stars needed if it's still locked.
- After winning, the win screen offers **Next level** (if unlocked) as well as Play Again and Menu (a new button that goes back to the title screen).

## Levels
| # | Level | Target | Par | Unlock | New idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Park | 10 | 5:00 | — | What exists today. Unchanged. |
| 2 | The Beach | 12 | 6:00 | ★ 2 | **Shallow water** slows everyone (×0.6). Timid dogs flee *into* it, so you need to cut off their escape. **Crabs** wander the waterline and nip at Luna (knockback, no points lost, scared off by a bark). Sand everywhere, with palm trees and rocks. |
| 3 | Town Square | 15 | 7:00 | ★ 5 | **Patrols:** aggressive dogs walk set routes along narrow streets instead of guarding yards, and chase when they spot Luna. Buildings block movement and sight lines (no noticing through walls). Lawns are small planters. |
| 4 | Picnic Party | 15 | 8:00 | ★ 8 | **Gather your friends:** befriended dogs follow Luna for as long as they're within 6 m (no 8 s timeout). You win by having **15 friends on the picnic blanket** at the same time. Dogs left behind wander off but stay befriended, and you can collect them again. |

- No way to lose in any level. The score still can't go below 0.
- Food, potty, the bark and the lure work in every level.

## Technical approach
1. **Turn the current world into level data.** Move `World`'s `YARDS`, `GARDENS`, `LAWNS`, prop counts, ground colours and border style into level definitions (`scripts/levels.gd`, extended from stage 1). `World.build(level, seed)` reads them. The Park's data must reproduce today's layout, which a test checks (same seed gives the same yards, gardens, lawns and dog count).
2. **Beach:** a `water` area list (rects/circles) with a speed multiplier applied in `Luna` and `Dog._move`, and a timid flee-target bias toward water. A `Crab` NPC: a small new model and a simple patrol/nip script (reuse the knockback in `Luna.hit`, with a new `costs_point=false` path).
3. **Town:** building blocks (boxes with simple roofs, built in code or as Blender props) as navmesh obstacles. `Dog` gets an optional `patrol: PackedVector3Array`, and an aggressive dog with a patrol walks it instead of wandering. Line of sight for noticing: a raycast against layer 1 before reacting.
4. **Picnic:** a `goal_area` in the level data. A `FOLLOW` variant with no timeout and a distance limit. Win when `friends inside goal_area >= target`. The HUD shows "Friends on blanket: N / 15".
5. **Level select and flow:** `Progress` stores stars per level, and `Levels.is_unlocked(id)` uses the total stars. `Main` reads `GameState.level_id`, set from the splash screen, and the win-panel buttons are wired up as above.

Build in this order: (1) → level select and flow → Beach → check how it feels → Town → Picnic. Each level ships on its own.

## Tests
- The Park data reproduces the current layout. All existing tests pass on the Park.
- Unlock rules at the star boundaries.
- Beach: Luna and dogs move slower in water, and a crab nip knocks Luna back without changing the score.
- Town: an aggressive dog follows its patrol, and it doesn't notice Luna behind a wall but does in the open.
- Picnic: follow persists within range and drops outside it, and the win triggers only when enough friends are on the blanket.

## Acceptance
- Each level has screenshots that read clearly at 200% zoom, and has been played through by hand at least once by the user before the next level starts.
- Each level has its own ADR.

## Open questions
- Do Beach and Town need their own music variations? Suggested: tempo or instrument changes to the same tune, generated by `make_sounds.py`.
- Should the Picnic target count dogs on the blanket at the same time (as written) or befriended overall? Simultaneous is more of a challenge but could be fiddly. Revisit after a prototype.
- Map size per level: keep 60×60 m, or make Town smaller and denser?
